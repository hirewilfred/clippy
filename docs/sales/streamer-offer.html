How it works

Four steps, about 10 minutes of your time a week

  1. Connect

    Send us your Twitch channel and link your TikTok and YouTube to our posting tool. We never ask for your passwords.

  2. We clip

    Within 24 hours of each stream, we pull the best moments and cut them.

  3. You approve

    Check the day's clips in one place. Approve, reject, or ask for a change.

  4. We post and report

    Approved clips go out on schedule. You get the numbers every month.
